# M7 test media attribution

The M7 research uses the original M1 synthetic fixtures and the licensed M4 portrait and mountain panorama. The portrait is NASA / Robert Markowitz's public-domain photograph of Commander Eileen Collins. The mountain panorama is by Hannes Röst and is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

The source files retain their recorded M4 transformations. M7 templates and screenshots show paint-by-numbers conversion, and the pixel comparison figure crops and juxtaposes generated outputs. Derivatives of the CC BY-SA panorama are offered under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms. These test media are excluded from the customer ZIP.
